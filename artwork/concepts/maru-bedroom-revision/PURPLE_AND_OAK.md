# Maru Tech: purple and oak

Color study after Ken's feedback on the bedroom revision. Use amethyst computer shells with plum shadows and lavender edge highlights, green CRT on the Core, and small blue controls. Mount the Press jaws on a real honey-oak drawer box with metal corners. The processor chip shares the purple accent. Keep thin copper drops and the thicker neutral gray trunk distinct. Same farm composition for comparison, but the board is a mockup, not a real game capture or an approved native sprite atlas.
